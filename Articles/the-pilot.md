# The Pilot

Behold the commencement of mine tale. The quest to enshrine all my knowledge, that it may endure beyond the confines of time and space, transcending the tumult of entropy's decay. In pursuit to fathom the very soul within, and to wrestle with the chaos that dwelleth therein. To cleanse the mind, to bring order unto thine self, and thus may I come to know well my might and my frailty.
